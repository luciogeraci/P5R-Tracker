Ryuji's confidant:
Unlocking: Very Start of the game, during the second kamoshida's castle visit
After this, you can find him by the stairwell on the second floor of Shujin Academy. If you don't have school that day, check the arcade in Shibuya instead.
Ryuji's Persona 5 Royal Confidant Rank 5 cannot be accessed until after 5/6, following the completion of the Kamoshida story arc.

April: 20, 25, or 27, May: 18, 25, or 27, June: 22 or 29, July: 1 or 6, August: 12 or 17, September: 21, October: 2, November: 2, 4, 9, 11, 16, or 30, December: 2, 7, or 9, January: 13

Kawakami's confidant
Unlocking: Two days after the Phantom Thieves finish Madarame’s Museum Palace, Joker will make nighttime plans with Ryuji and Mishima anytime after 5/24.
use the yellow phone on the counter at Leblanc to call Kawakami at nighttime on Fridays and Saturdays.
Deadline: Kawakami’s Confidant in Persona 5 Royal will lock on 11/17 for story progression. While she becomes available again at the beginning of January, her perks are most helpful while you’re in the main part of the story.
Confidant type: Temperance
Kawakami’s Confidant, Rank 8 is only available on school nights. Part two takes place the following day at school, but it will not take up Joker’s free time that day. You receive the “A Teacher Maid to Suffer” Mementos Request during this scene
Kawakami's Confidant, Rank 9 Is locked until you complete the “A Teacher Maid to Suffer” Mementos Request for Kawakami, and she will only call you afterwards if you choose to stay friends. Additionally, this event will only be available on schooldays.
Unless you've kept one on you, be sure to grab a Temperance Persona on 9/6 before leaving for the trip - the Velvet Room is inaccessible on vacation!
You can spend time with Kawakami:
At the Ueno Museum.
At the buffet in Shibuya, if you stayed friends.
On the Ferris wheel in Odaiba, if you're dating.
In the attic at Leblanc, also if you're dating.
